# Marketeer 1.0.95

- Hover the selected item in Market History to see its tooltip and hold Shift for market details. A matching bag stack supplies stack values when available.
- Your Auctions now shows matched commodity units ahead of each listing, based on auction search result order. A dash indicates the value is unavailable.
- Hover an item in Your Auctions and hold Shift for market details using the listing's quantity.
