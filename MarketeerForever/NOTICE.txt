Vendor locations: derived from QuestieDB Forever 1.1.3
(https://github.com/Questie/QuestieDB); see data/questiedb/ for licensing,

Monster drops, item sources, locations, and observed drop rates: Wowhead

(https://github.com/cmangos/classic-db), GPL-3.0; not verified for Forever.
The reference SQL, generator, and license are included in Source/ClassicDB/.
