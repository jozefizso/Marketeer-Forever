Local vendor locations are derived from the QuestieDB 1.1.3 Forever release:
https://github.com/Questie/QuestieDB/releases/tag/v1.1.3

The extraction preserves item vendor IDs, NPC names, faction friendliness,
Forever spawn coordinates and area-to-map mappings. See LICENSE.md and
gpl-3.0.md for the upstream project's licensing and attribution information.
The extraction tool is Tools/extract-vendor-locations.py in the source tree.
